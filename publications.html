Submitted

  • Kantor, A. Choosing content is choosing a mechanism: How information features shape who joins online discussions and how much they elaborate.Separates two ways a news article’s information shapes discussion: changing who takes part, and changing how the same people engage. Using within-commenter variation across about 4,600 NYT stories, it finds that who takes part matters more, and that a single feature can work through both pathways in opposite directions.

Working Papers

  • Kantor, A. The narrative ratchet: Online information environments and the asymmetric adoption of inflation explanations.Classifies the causal explanations for inflation in New York Times coverage and about 620,000 reader comments. Explanations that blame identifiable actors are adopted quickly and decay slowly, regardless of the evidence against them.

  • Kantor, A. The last mile: Information design after toxicity removal.Shows that the information features of a news article predict the quality of the discussion that remains after moderation, across 29 Perspective API dimensions and about 620,000 NYT comments.

  • Kantor, A. Open data that cannot be told: Essential statistical opacity in international assessments.PISA’s released scores preserve gaps between groups but understate differences in who is affected, by about a fifth in the Israeli 2022 and 2025 samples. Claims that exceed the data come mainly from those who interpret the results for the public, not from news reports. The companion site opisa.org presents the 2025 results with their margins of error.
